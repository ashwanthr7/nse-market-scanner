# Swing Trading — Delivery Ratio Scanner v9

Scanner-only build. The Market Lens/query builder, fundamentals, and NSE sector filtering have been removed.

Features:
- Full NSE equity universe
- Shared/cached NSE daily data engine
- Delivery Ratio: UP-day deliverable quantity / DOWN-day deliverable quantity
- Period selection from 1 month to 5 years
- Built-in ratio conditions: >2.00x, >1.50x, >1.20x, 1.20x–1.50x, <0.80x
- Multiple conditions can be selected together
- Grouped results with COPY and COPY ALL
